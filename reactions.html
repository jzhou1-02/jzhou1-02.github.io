Every reaction we’ve run and retired.

Power sources move the car, stopping reactions stops the motor. Keep in mind each status: proven has raced, experimental was or is tested, retired means DONT REPEAT HISTORY.

StopProven

Iodine clock (thiosulfate)

2 S₂O₃²⁻ + I₂ → S₄O₆²⁻ + 2 I⁻ · then starch + I₂ → dark

Solution goes dark when thiosulfate runs out; a photoresistor sees the change and opens the motor relay. Stop time scales with [S₂O₃²⁻]. The calibration curve is parabolic over our range.

Cars: II, III, IV · Calibrate at venue temperature — see 2025 post-mortem
PowerProven

Zn/Cu galvanic stack

Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu · E°cell ≈ 1.10 V

Reliable and boring in the best way. Six cells in series gave stable voltage under load for the full run window.

Cars: II, III · ~1.0 V/cell under load
PowerExperimental

Aluminum–air battery

4 Al + 3 O₂ + 6 H₂O → 4 Al(OH)₃ · E°cell ≈ 2.7 V (theor.)

Much better energy density than Zn/Cu, which is the whole pitch for Car IV. Real cell voltage sags well below theoretical and the anodes passivate. Current work is on electrolyte choice and mesh cathodes.

Car: IV (current) · Bench best: ~1.2 V/cell under load
StopExperimental

Vitamin C clock

ascorbic acid + I₂ → dehydroascorbic + 2 HI

A gentler iodine clock variant using ascorbic acid as the reducing agent, friendlier EDP profile. Timing is less sharp than thiosulfate so far. The color transition is more gradual, bad for photo receiver.

Status: bench only · Needs a threshold circuit, not a simple divider
PowerRetired

Pressure car (baking soda + vinegar)

NaHCO₃ + CH₃COOH → CO₂↑ + H₂O + NaOAc

Car I's propulsion. Fun, fast, wildly inconsistent. Pressure release depends on mixing, temperature, and luck. Also increasingly constrained by competition pressure-vessel rules. Retired.

Car: I · Retired 2023 · Reason: repeatability + rules
StopRetired

Mechanical timer stop

no reaction, wind-up timer opens the circuit

Not a reaction at all, which is exactly why it's retired: competition scoring rewards chemical stopping mechanisms, and the timer drifted with spring tension.

Car: I · Retired 2023
